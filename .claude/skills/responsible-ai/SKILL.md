# Responsible AI

- AI outputs are suggestions, not authoritative decisions.
- Never automatically downgrade serious safety reports.
- Preserve original reporter descriptions.
- Record model suggestions and human overrides.
- Identify uncertainty explicitly.
- Test across languages and writing styles.
- Never assume low confidence means low risk.
- Require approval for consequential actions.
-Require human approval before going to next page or screen.
- Require human approval before outcome letters are sent.
- Evaluate errors, bias and unsafe recommendations.
- Never use confidential data with unapproved public AI services.
