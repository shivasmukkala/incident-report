# Responsible AI Framework

## Principles

AI must assist people rather than replace accountable decision-makers.

## Permitted AI Assistance

- Incident category suggestions
- Similarity-based duplicate suggestions
- Investigation note summaries
- Outcome letter drafting
- Translation
- Speech-to-text transcription and suggested form-field extraction
- Contextual follow-up questions to clarify missing information

## Human Approval

- The reporter reviews and approves AI-extracted information before submission.
- The reporter decides when to submit the incident.
- Case handlers confirm or change priority and category.
- Case handlers approve assignments.
- Potential duplicates require human review before closure.
- Authorised personnel approve final resolution and closure.
- Outcome letters require human approval before dispatch.

## Voice Input Safeguards

- Explain when microphone access and speech processing are used.
- Preserve the original transcript for review.
- Allow reporters to edit extracted information.
- Never invent details that were not spoken.
- Leave uncertain information blank or mark it for confirmation.
- Provide a complete text-based alternative to voice input.
- Apply an approved retention policy to audio recordings and transcripts.

## Safety

Never automatically downgrade a serious incident based on
model confidence, language quality or incomplete information.

Use conservative deterministic escalation rules.

## Fairness

Test reports written in:
- Clear English
- Poor or informal English
- Different languages
- Short descriptions
- Incomplete descriptions

## Transparency

Record:
- Whether AI was used
- Type of AI assistance
- Model and version where available
- Suggested result
- Human decision
- Override reason where applicable

Disclose AI-assisted drafting and translation to reviewers
and recipients as appropriate.

## Data Protection

Use fictional or approved anonymised data.

Do not send confidential data to public AI services.

## Evaluation

Measure:
- Classification agreement with human reviewers
- Duplicate suggestion quality
- Translation review outcomes
- Human override frequency
- Unsafe recommendation cases

AI output must be treated as untrusted until reviewed.
